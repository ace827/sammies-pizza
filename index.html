Our Story

Real Food,
Made by Real People

Sammie started with one simple belief: that Manchester deserved a neighborhood pizza place that actually cared — about the ingredients, about the craft, and about the people coming through the door. Six years and countless pies later, that belief hasn't changed one bit.

  • Every pizza is made fresh to order — never pre-made or frozen
  • Quality toppings sourced for flavor, not just cost
  • Our famous 10" chocolate chip cookie is baked fresh every day
  • A family operation — Sammie and her team personally prepare every order
  • Fast, friendly carry-out — your order is always ready when promised

Simple & Easy

How to Order

No apps, no fuss. Just call, and we'll have your food hot and ready in minutes.

01
Call Us

Pick up the phone and dial (937) 549-0003. We're available Mon–Sat from 4:00–9:00 PM. Tell us exactly what you want — we'll walk you through the full menu if you need help.

02
We Make It Fresh

Your order goes straight to the kitchen — made from scratch with fresh ingredients. No pre-made pies sitting around. Most orders are ready in 15–25 minutes. We'll confirm your pickup time on the call.

03
Pick It Up Hot

Swing by 403 Jack Roush Way and pick up your order at the counter. It'll be fresh, hot, and exactly as ordered. We accept cash and all major cards.
