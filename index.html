Our Story

Real Food,
Made by Real People

Sammie started with one simple belief: that Manchester deserved a neighborhood spot that actually cared - about the ingredients, about the craft, and about the people coming through the door. Six years and countless satisfied customers later, that belief hasn't changed one bit.

  • Every order is made fresh - never pre-made or sitting under a heat lamp
  • Quality ingredients sourced for flavor, not just cost
  • A full menu: sandwiches, pasta, calzones, chicken tenders and more
  • A family operation - Sammie and her team personally prepare every order
  • Fast, friendly pickup - your order is always ready when promised
